---
name: Legacy property ownership
description: Why older property ads need explicit ownership reconciliation instead of automatic matching.
---

Do not assign legacy property ownership or delete legacy published ads by matching title, location, phone, or images to registrations.

**Why:** Earlier approval created random listing IDs and copied mutable fields without retaining a registration or owner reference. Review audit history proves a registration was approved but cannot establish which published listing it created. Guessing can disclose notifications to the wrong account or remove another ad.

**How to apply:** New approvals retain exact ownership links. Older unmapped ads need explicit, verified reconciliation; keep them ownerless rather than inventing historical ownership. Treat notification contact events as button presses, not proof that a call or WhatsApp conversation occurred.
