---
name: Homepage ad placement
description: Placement and rotation behavior for customer-facing paid campaigns.
---

Eligible paid campaigns appear directly below the main home hero/search box. If several campaigns match the current location, the placement cycles through them every five seconds.

**Why:** This is the primary promotional slot requested for the customer home experience; keeping one visible card avoids pushing the marketplace content too far down.

**How to apply:** Preserve the shared sponsored-card treatment, fetch at most five eligible campaigns, rotate one card in place, and record an impression whenever the campaign changes.

Campaigns may target multiple official Omani wilayats. Location aliases such as Al Khuwair must resolve alongside the official wilayat (Bawshar), and service labels in Arabic or English must normalize to the app's stable service IDs before matching.
